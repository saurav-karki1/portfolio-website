Core Features

Every capability built around the real daily workflow of Nepali farm owners, tea garden managers, and agricultural labor contractors.

All Features

Offline SQLite Database

Your data lives on your device. Zero internet required. Built on Drift ORM with SQLite, it works in Ilam tea gardens, Jhapa potato farms, and Panchthar cardamom slopes with no cellular signal.

SQLite via Drift ORM Encrypted local storage Works at altitude

Exact Integer Paisa Arithmetic

Every Nepal Rupee split into 100 paisa. Integer-only math eliminates the floating-point errors that cause wage disputes between landowners and workers.

1 NPR = 100 paisa Zero rounding errors Audited totals

Bikram Sambat Calendar

Native Nepali calendar support. Month-based ledger grouping from Baisakh (बैशाख) to Chaitra (चैत्र). Your records match how Nepal counts time.

Full BS date support Monthly settlement periods Year-end reports

Worker Profiles & Rate Overrides

Create profiles for each khetala worker with a default daily rate. Override the rate per entry for seasonal or task-based work like tea picking by kilogram.

Per-worker default rates Entry-level rate override Multi-worker estates

Audited Voids

Mistakes happen. Voided entries are preserved as an audited record (never silently deleted). Full history, full accountability for dispute-proof bookkeeping.

Void preserved in history No silent deletes Dispute-proof ledger

PDF Statement Generation

Generate professional A4 PDF statements on-device. Share directly via WhatsApp or email. 25-row chunks for multi-page histories.

A4 format On-device generation Share via any app

Daily Reminders

Two daily reminder prompts at 5 PM and 8 PM to log the day’s field work before it’s forgotten. Scheduled locally on your device with no server required.

5 PM & 8 PM alerts Local scheduling No push server

Cloud Synchronization

Cloud-backed agricultural accounting with Google Cloud Firestore. Automatically synchronizes pending records when connected, during data mutations, or upon manual trigger, providing multi-device continuity across your household.

Same-account household sync Multi-device continuity Strict privacy rules
