Transparent Accounting for Every Farm Worker

Inside the worker ledger screen. Every agricultural laborer receives an independent ledger account tracking earnings, advances, and settlements with mathematical certainty.

Worker Profiles & Settled Status

Dedicated accounts for each laborer. The instant settled badge (Settled / साफ) confirms zero outstanding wage debt at the end of each harvest or payment cycle.

Running Ledger & Opening Balances

Three-way balance breakdown: Opening Balance (खाता शुरुवात), Total Earnings (जम्मा कमाई), and Total Payments/Advances (जम्मा भुक्तानी) computed with exact integer paisa arithmetic.

On-Device Statement PDF Export

Generate verifiable A4 wage statements directly on the phone. Includes 25-row chunking, Bikram Sambat date stamps, and one-tap sharing to WhatsApp or local printers.

Core capabilities

Built for Nepali Farms

Every feature designed around the real workflow of tea garden managers, potato farm owners, and agricultural labor contractors in eastern Nepal.

Offline SQLite Database

Your data lives on your device. Zero internet required. Built on Drift ORM with SQLite, it works in Ilam tea gardens, Jhapa potato farms, and Panchthar cardamom slopes with no cellular signal.

Exact Integer Paisa Arithmetic

Every Nepal Rupee split into 100 paisa. Integer-only math eliminates the floating-point errors that cause wage disputes between landowners and workers.

Bikram Sambat Calendar

Native Nepali calendar support. Month-based ledger grouping from Baisakh (बैशाख) to Chaitra (चैत्र). Your records match how Nepal counts time.

Worker Profiles & Rates

Create profiles for each khetala worker with a default daily rate. Override the rate per entry for seasonal or task-based work like tea picking by kilogram.

Audited Voids

Mistakes happen. Voided entries are preserved as an audited record (never silently deleted). Full history, full accountability for dispute-proof bookkeeping.

PDF Statement Generation

Generate professional A4 PDF statements on-device. Share directly via WhatsApp or email. 25-row chunks for multi-page histories.

Daily Reminders

Two daily reminder prompts at 5 PM and 8 PM to log the day’s field work before it’s forgotten. Scheduled locally with no server required.

Cloud Synchronization

Cloud Firestore synchronizes your household ledger automatically. Access records across devices and protect farm history if your phone is replaced.
