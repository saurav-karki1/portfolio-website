Answers to common questions about offline usage, data privacy, device compatibility, and accounting accuracy.

The app works offline in remote fields using local SQLite storage. All worker profiles, daily picking records, advances, and ledger calculations operate directly without cellular signal or Wi-Fi. When connectivity returns, pending records synchronize with Cloud Firestore.
Digital Khetala Hisab-Kitab combines local-first Drift SQLite storage with Cloud Firestore synchronization. You can record work and payments offline without internet. When your phone connects to the network, pending changes automatically synchronize with your private household in Cloud Firestore, enabling multi-device access and protecting your records if your phone is replaced.
Because your household records are synchronized with Cloud Firestore, you simply sign in to the same account on your new Android phone to restore your complete farm ledger. Additionally, you can create offline JSON backups with SHA-256 verification at any time for secondary local archiving.

Standard software often calculates currency using floating-point numbers, which introduces subtle rounding errors (e.g., 0.1 + 0.2 = 0.30000000000000004). Digital Khetala represents all monetary values as integer paisa (1 NPR = 100 paisa). Every calculation is performed with exact integer math, ensuring totals on paper receipts and digital screens always balance down to the last paisa.
When an entry is entered by mistake, the app does not silently delete the database row. Instead, it marks the entry as "voided" and records the timestamp and reason. This maintains an immutable historical audit trail, preventing accusations of tampering or accidental loss of record history.
