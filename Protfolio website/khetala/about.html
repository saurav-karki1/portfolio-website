About Digital Khetala

Why we built an offline accounting app for Nepal's fields and farms.

The Problem

Every monsoon season, rain-soaked paper khata notebooks destroy months of labor records across Nepal’s tea gardens and farm estates. A single downpour in Ilam can wash out an entire season of picking records, triggering wage disputes that harm both workers and landowners.

Digital Khetala Hisab-Kitab replaces the paper khata with an offline-first Android app that works in remote fields with no internet connection, built specifically for Nepal’s tea estates in Ilam, potato farms in Jhapa, and cardamom slopes in Panchthar.

What the App Does

Eight verified features built for real farm accounting needs.

  • Offline-first SQLite database: All data stored locally via Drift ORM. No internet required for any core feature.
  • Integer paisa arithmetic: 1 NPR = 100 paisa. All calculations use integer math, with zero rounding errors, ever.
  • Bikram Sambat calendar: Native Nepali calendar support. Records grouped by BS month, Baisakh to Chaitra.
  • Worker profiles: Individual profiles per khetala worker with name, default wage, and complete entry history. Unlimited profiles.
  • Audited void entries: Corrections are logged, never silently deleted. Full audit trail for every change.
  • A4 PDF statements: Generate and share printable statements directly from your phone. No internet needed.
  • Daily reminders: Optional notifications at 5 PM and 8 PM to prompt daily record entry.
  • Cloud synchronization: Google Cloud Firestore synchronizes household records across devices, protecting data if a phone is lost or upgraded.
